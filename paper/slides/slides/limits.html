How far to trust this

Honest limits

Small samples

60 conversations per condition (30 for Sonnet). Gaps under about 15 points can be luck.

A simulated customer

About 1 in 4 sampled failures came from the customer going off-script. It hits every condition alike.

One domain

Airline support only, and its tasks rarely tempt a model to break rules.

Rules by construction

Zero violations at H2 and H3 is partly built in: the audit uses the same rules H2 enforces.

Gate limits

Free-text rules (a valid insurance reason) can’t be checked in code. Several changes in one message are checked against the old state.

Point in time

Model versions and hosts pinned in November 2026; results may not hold for later releases.

Harness ladder · τ²-bench airline

None of these is hidden in the write-up. Every conversation has its full trace, so anyone can recheck a number.
